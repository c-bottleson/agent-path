# Agent Path — AI Assessment Questionnaire
## For use during discovery call or voice agent interview

---

## SECTION 1: CORE QUESTIONS (Ask Every Time)

These establish the baseline. Every assessment starts here regardless of industry.

### Business Profile
1. What does your business do in one sentence?
2. How many employees do you have? (Full-time, part-time, contractors)
3. How long have you been in business?
4. What's your approximate annual revenue? (Ranges are fine: under $500K, $500K-$2M, $2M-$10M, $10M+)
5. How many locations do you have?

### Current Tools & Tech Stack
6. What software or tools do you use daily to run the business? (CRM, accounting, email, scheduling, etc.)
7. Are there any tools you pay for but barely use?
8. Do you have a website? Does it generate leads or just exist as a brochure?
9. How do you communicate internally — email, Slack, texts, carrier pigeon?
10. Do you use any automation today? (Zapier, scheduled emails, auto-replies, anything)

### Time & Pain
11. What task takes the most time in your week that doesn't directly make you money?
12. What's the one repetitive task you wish someone else would just handle?
13. Where do things fall through the cracks most often?
14. What keeps you up at night about the business?
15. If you could wave a magic wand and automate one thing, what would it be?

### Revenue & Growth
16. How do most of your customers find you today? (Referrals, Google, social, ads, walk-in)
17. How fast do you respond to a new lead? (Minutes, hours, days, "when I get to it")
18. What happens to a lead if you're busy or after hours?
19. What's your biggest bottleneck to growing revenue right now?
20. Do you follow up with past customers? How often?

### Team & Operations
21. What tasks do your highest-paid people do that a lower-paid person or tool could handle?
22. How long does it take to get a new hire up to speed?
3. What happens when someone is out — does work stop or pile up?
24. Are there tasks only one person knows how to do? (Key-person risk)
25. Where do handoffs between people or departments break down?

## SECTION 2: MIDDLE QUESTIONS (Industry-Specific)

Pick the block that matches the client's industry. These dig into the workflows unique to their vertical.

### HOME SERVICES (HVAC, Plumbing, Roofing, Landscaping, Cleaning)
M1. How do you currently schedule jobs and dispatch technicians?
M2. How do customers book — phone call, website, referral only?
M3. What happens when a call comes in and nobody answers?
M4. How do you estimate jobs? On-site visits, photos, flat rates?
M5. Do you send reminders before appointments? How?
M6. How do you collect reviews after a job?
M7. How do you handle seasonal demand swings — too busy in summer, dead in winter?
M8. Do you track which marketing channels actually send you jobs?
M9. How do you manage inventory of parts and supplies?
M10. What's your process for following up on estimates that didn't close?

### HEALTHCARE & WELLNESS (Dental, Chiropractic, Therapy, Med Spas, Veterinary)
M1. How do patients currently book appointments?
M2. What's your no-show rate, and what do you do about it?
M3. How do you handle intake forms and patient paperwork?
M4. Do you send appointment reminders? How far in advance?
M5. How do you manage patient follow-up after treatment?
M6. What's your process for handling insurance verification?
M7. Do you collect and respond to online reviews?
M8. How do patients find you — referrals, insurance directories, Google?
M9. What happens when the office is closed and someone calls?
M10. How do you track patient satisfaction or outcomes?

### PROFESSIONAL SERVICES (Law, Accounting, Consulting, Financial Planning)
M1. How do potential clients find and evaluate you before reaching out?
M2. What's your intake/onboarding process for new clients?
M3. How do you track billable hours or project milestones?
M4. What percentage of your time is admin vs. client-facing work?
M5. How do you handle document management and retrieval?
M6. Do you send regular updates to clients, or only when they ask?
M7. How do you manage your calendar and avoid double-booking?
M8. What compliance or regulatory requirements constrain your processes?
M9. How do you generate proposals or engagement letters?
M10. Do you have a referral program or system for generating referral business?

### RESTAURANTS & HOSPITALITY (Restaurants, Catering, Hotels, Event Venues)
M1. How do customers make reservations or place orders?
M2. How do you manage staffing schedules?
M3. What's your process for handling online reviews (Yelp, Google, TripAdvisor)?
M4. How do you manage inventory and reduce food waste?
M5. Do you have a loyalty or repeat-customer program?
M6. How do you handle catering or large-event inquiries?
M7. What's your process for social media and marketing?
M8. How do you handle complaints or negative reviews?
M9. Do you offer online ordering or delivery? How is it managed?
M10. How do you track which menu items are most/least profitable?

### E-COMMERCE & RETAIL (Online stores, Boutiques, DTC Brands)
M1. What platform is your store built on? (Shopify, WooCommerce, custom)
M2. How do you handle customer support — email, chat, phone?
M3. What's your return/exchange process?
M4. How do you recover abandoned carts?
M5. How do you manage inventory across channels (website, Amazon, in-store)?
M6. What's your email marketing setup? How often do you send?
M7. How do you generate product descriptions and marketing copy?
M8. How do you handle product photography and visual content?
M9. What analytics do you track, and what do you actually use to make decisions?
M10. How do you manage reviews and user-generated content?

### REAL ESTATE & PROPERTY (Agents, Property Management, Brokers)
M1. How do you generate and qualify leads?
M2. How fast do you respond to inbound inquiries?
M3. What's your process from first contact to closing?
M4. How do you manage your listings across platforms?
M5. Do you use a CRM? Is the data actually up to date?
M6. How do you handle tenant communication (if property management)?
M7. What's your process for showing properties — scheduling, follow-up?
M8. How do you create marketing materials for listings?
M9. How do you stay top-of-mind with past clients for referrals?
M10. What admin tasks eat up time that should be spent with clients?

### CONSTRUCTION & CONTRACTING (General contractors, Specialty trades, Builders)
M1. How do you estimate projects and generate bids?
M2. How do you manage project timelines and subcontractor coordination?
M3. What's your change order process?
M4. How do you track job costs vs. estimates?
M5. How do you communicate progress to clients during a project?
M6. What's your process for collecting payments — deposits, draws, final?
M7. How do you handle permit and inspection scheduling?
M8. How do you manage safety documentation and compliance?
M9. How do you generate leads for new projects?
M10. What happens between projects — how do you keep the pipeline full?

## SECTION 3: ENDING QUESTIONS (Ask Every Time)

These close the interview and give you the data needed to build the upsell path.

### AI Exposure & Openness
E1. Has anyone on your team tried using AI tools on their own? (ChatGPT, Copilot, anything)
E2. If yes, what happened? Was it useful or a waste of time?
E3. If no, what's held you back?
E4. On a scale of 1-10, how open are you to adopting new technology?
E5. Have you been burned by a tech purchase before? What happened?

### Priorities & Budget
E6. If you could fix ONE thing about your operations in the next 90 days, what would it be?
E7. What would that fix be worth to you in saved time or new revenue?
E8. Do you have a budget set aside for technology or process improvements?
E9. Who else would be involved in a decision to adopt new tools or processes?
E10. Is there a deadline or event driving this? (New location, busy season, key hire leaving)

### Closing & Next Steps
E11. Is there anything about your business I didn't ask about that you think is important?
E12. What would make this assessment most valuable to you?
E13. How do you prefer to receive the report — slides, PDF, live walkthrough?

## INTERVIEW TIPS

- **Let them talk.** The best data comes from follow-up questions, not the list itself.
- **Mirror their language.** If they say "jobs" not "work orders," use their words.
- **Quantify everything.** "That takes a lot of time" → "About how many hours per week?"
- **Note emotional signals.** When they sigh, laugh, or say "ugh" — that's the pain point.
- **Don't pitch solutions.** The report does that. The interview is about listening.
- **Record if possible.** Transcription feeds directly into the Claude prompt.
- **Aim for 25-35 minutes.** Short enough to respect their time, long enough to get depth.
