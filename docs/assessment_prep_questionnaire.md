# AI Assessment Prep Questionnaire

Send after purchase or before a scheduled assessment.

## Copy-paste message

Thanks for booking the AI Assessment. Before the call, send whatever you can answer below. Bullet points are fine. Messy is fine.

1. What does the business do?
2. Rough team size and revenue range?
3. What software/tools do you use now? CRM, scheduling, email, QuickBooks, spreadsheets, forms, etc.
4. What happens when a new lead/customer comes in?
5. What gets missed when the team is busy?
6. What do you copy/paste, retype, chase, check, or remind people about every week?
7. What reports do you wish you had but never make?
8. What is a missed lead/job/customer worth roughly?
9. What would make this assessment worth it 30 days from now?
10. Anything you already know you do NOT want AI touching?

If you do not know an answer, skip it. The goal is to make the call useful, not create homework.
