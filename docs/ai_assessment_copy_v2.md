Keep the positioning: this is not an "AI strategy" product. It is a paid teardown that tells an owner whether AI is worth touching at all.

Change the language from anti-consultant broadside to buyer-specific certainty. The current page is close, but it repeats the same promise too many times: no hype, no fluff, honest answer. We need more proof of what they receive and what decision it helps them make.

## Problem section rewrite

### Heading

### Body
You need to know whether any of this applies to your business.

Most owners are stuck between two bad choices: ignore AI completely, or let someone sell them a giant transformation project. The Assessment is the middle path. We look at how work actually moves through your business and find the places where automation can pay for itself.

## Comparison cards rewrite

### What most consultants sell
A roadmap. A workshop. A long PDF. A vague promise that your business needs to "embrace AI."

### What this is
A practical teardown of your operations with costs, savings, and a short list of fixes worth doing first.

### What ChatGPT gives you
Generic ideas that sound plausible because they apply to almost every business.

### What the Assessment gives you
Specific recommendations based on your numbers, tools, team, and bottlenecks.

Example: "You are losing around 8 after-hours leads per month. If 3 would have booked at $700/job, a $50/month call responder is worth testing."

## What you get rewrite

### 1. Workflow teardown
A call of 60 minutes or less where we walk through how work actually happens: leads, scheduling, admin, handoffs, invoicing, follow-up, reporting, and the tools you already use.

### 2. AI opportunity map
A written blueprint ranking your best automation opportunities by expected savings, cost, difficulty, and speed to implement.

### 3. First 3 fixes
Not a list of 25 ideas. The top 3 fixes worth doing first, with the tool, estimated setup cost, monthly cost, and why each one matters.

### 4. Go/no-go verdict
A plain answer: build now, wait, or skip it. If AI will not meaningfully help, the report will say so.

## FAQ rewrite

### What if AI cannot help my business?
Then the recommendation will say that. You still leave knowing not to spend $5,000-$50,000 chasing a bad fit.

### Do I need to understand AI?
No. The report translates everything into hours, dollars, tools, and next steps.

### Is this a sales pitch for implementation?
No. The Assessment stands alone. If an implementation makes sense, I will show the path. If it does not, I will say that too.

### What do you need from me?
A working knowledge of how your business runs: how leads come in, what tools you use, where work gets stuck, and what tasks waste the most time.

### What happens after the Assessment?
You can implement the blueprint yourself, hand it to someone else, hire us, or do nothing. The deliverable is useful either way.
