Who to call first

Property managers who want more doors.

Prioritize small-to-mid property management companies. They feel owner-acquisition pain, but are not so large that every process is locked behind corporate layers.

  • Call: 50–500 managed doors.
  • Call: local/regional PM firms.
  • Call: companies advertising owner services.
  • Call: owner/operator visible on site.
  • Avoid: giant national franchises first.
  • Avoid: firms with no PM focus.
  • Avoid: pure realtors with occasional rentals.
  • Avoid: anyone asking for guaranteed closed clients.

Call script

Open short. Ask one real question.

“Hey, this is Nick with Agent Path. I’m testing a practical lead radar for property managers. We find local owner/rental opportunities that may be worth outreach — things like stale listings, out-of-state owners, weak rental marketing, or owner-managed rentals. I made a small sample for property managers in your market. Who handles new owner acquisition for you?”

If they engage

“The first sample is free. If the leads look useful, we can refresh it weekly for your territory. The important part: we do not sell the same opportunities to direct competitors.”

If they ask what AI does

“AI is mostly behind the scenes. The product is not software you have to learn. It is a weekly list of owner/rental opportunities with the reason they may be worth contacting and what to say.”

Objection replies

Keep it plain. Do not argue.

“We already buy leads.”

“Makes sense. This is different from generic shared lead marketplaces. The goal is a curated list of owner/rental situations in your actual territory, with the reason they may be worth outreach. If the sample is not useful, don’t use it.”

“Can you guarantee new owners?”

“No. I would not promise that honestly. We guarantee the research/feed and territory protection. The sample exists so you can judge lead quality before paying.”

“Is this spam?”

“No. We are not blasting homeowners. We identify public business opportunities and give you a suggested human outreach angle. You decide who to contact and how.”

“Send me information.”

“I can. The useful thing is the sample page, not a brochure. I’ll send 5–10 examples. If those are weak, you’ll know immediately. What email should I send it to?”

“We’re not interested.”

“No problem. Before I let you go — is owner acquisition already handled well, or is it just not a priority this month?”

“How much?”

“The sample is free. If it is useful and you want the weekly protected territory feed, it is $799/month. Same leads are not sent to direct competitors.”

Close + payment

If they want it now, close the territory hold.

Use this when they say the sample looks useful or they want to reserve the market.

“The clean next step is to hold the territory and start the weekly feed. It’s $799/month. We’ll send the first full batch, lock those leads from competitors for 90 days, and review quality after the first week. If the first full batch is not useful, we cancel before the next month.”
