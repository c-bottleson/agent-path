Owner Lead Radar — how to run the test

The point of this test is not to convince a property manager that every record is perfect. The point is to show a balanced sample of Denver owner/rental signals and learn whether at least 2–3 are worth checking further. Send the email first so the buyer has context, then call within 10 minutes while the sample is fresh.

Open CRMOpen sample

Sequence

  1. Email first. Use the CRM email button or copy the draft.
  2. Call within 10 minutes. Say you just sent the sample and ask who reviews owner acquisition opportunities.
  3. Ask one useful question: “Do at least 2–3 of these look worth checking further?”
  4. Capture category feedback: recent sale, small multifamily, entity owner, out-of-state owner, high-value asset, or none.
  5. If yes: offer to reserve the Denver territory at $799/month for the protected weekly feed.

Email script

Subject: 25 Denver owner/rental signals worth checking? Hi {company}, I’m testing a Denver owner-lead radar for property managers. I pulled 25 sample property signals from public Denver records — recent-sale absentee owners, small multifamily, entity-owned rentals, out-of-state owners, and high-value assets. I would not expect every record to be useful. The test is simple: are at least 2–3 worth checking further? Sample: https://agent-path.vercel.app/radar/denver-sample/ If 2–3 look worth a closer look, I can reserve a Denver territory and send a protected weekly feed. Worth a quick look? Nick Agent Path

Call script

Hi, this is Nick with Agent Path. I just sent a short email with a Denver owner/rental sample. We’re testing whether property managers want a weekly radar of public-record signals — recent-sale absentee owners, small multifamily, LLC-owned rentals, out-of-state owners. The sample has 25 records. I’m not claiming every one is a lead. I’m trying to learn whether 2 or 3 are worth checking further. Who’s the right person to review owner acquisition opportunities?

Recording calls

Operational rule: disclose recording at the start and only continue recording if they agree. Minnesota may allow one-party consent, but outbound calls can cross state lines and company policies vary. The clean line is: “Quick note: I may record this call so I can capture feedback accurately. Is that okay?” If they say no, do not record — take notes.

What not to say

  • Do not say these owners are ready to hire a property manager.
  • Do not say the sample includes owner phone/email. It does not yet.
  • Do not sell “unlimited leads.” Sell a protected weekly researched feed.
