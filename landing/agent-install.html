Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. We set everything up together on a video call. You just need to be able to open a browser and follow along.

What are the ongoing costs?

Two things: ~$6.50/mo to OVHcloud for the server, and ~$5-15/mo for the AI API (OpenRouter). That's it. No subscription to us.

What can the agent actually build?

Booking systems, intake forms, social media content, follow-up emails, simple dashboards, landing pages, and more. It builds whatever your business needs — you just describe the problem.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is a chatbot you visit. Your agent is an employee that works for you 24/7. It knows your business, remembers everything, and actually builds tools — not just gives advice.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your agent lives on YOUR server. We don't have access to it after the install. Your data never touches our systems.

What if I need help after the install?

Your agent is smart enough to fix most things itself. For bigger changes, you can message us or book the 3-Month Review Pack.
